About

I am a first-year PhD student in the Department of Sociology at Florida State University. My work examines child welfare systems and postsecondary education as sites where resources flow into institutional structures and are converted into classificatory logics that generate durable inequalities for the populations moving through them.

I ask how institutions come to treat certain outcomes as acceptable losses rather than failures of design, and what it would mean to take seriously that the sorting was never inevitable. I use quantitative modeling of administrative and survey data alongside qualitative accounts from those who move through these systems.

Before beginning the PhD, I completed dual bachelor's degrees in Sociology and Psychology at Florida State University, graduating Magna Cum Laude with honors in both.
