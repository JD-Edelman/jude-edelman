Jude D. Edelman

PhD Student, Sociology — Florida State University

Broadly, my work interrogates the distinct mechanisms that make the top and bottom of the class distribution especially sticky and increasingly polarized, producing little mobility across the life course and between generations. I am currently working on projects that examine the child welfare system and postsecondary education as sites of potential mobility, where structural and discursive factors instead produce durable inequalities for the populations moving through them.

About

I am a first-year PhD student in the Department of Sociology at Florida State University.
